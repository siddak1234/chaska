The Lead

Generations of home cooking, now serving Frisco

Chaska serves desi food with a Punjabi tadka, cooked from family recipes that were passed down by hand rather than written down. Authentic home flavors, made the way maa used to make them, alongside a few Indo-fusion dishes of our own.

Everything is served family style, meant for the middle of the table.

The Family

Recipes taught by hand, kept by memory

Owner Ronika Singh learned this food in her family's kitchen, where recipes were measured in handfuls and pinches and passed from one generation to the next. Chaska is her way of bringing that table to Frisco.

The family is all here, including Snoopy, the house shih tzu and honorary sous chef.
