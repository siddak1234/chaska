The Lead

Three generations at one stove, now cooking in Dallas

Chaska serves desi food with a Punjabi tadka, cooked from family recipes that were never written down. They were watched, tasted and corrected across three generations, and they arrive at your table unchanged: dal simmered overnight, saag stirred through the afternoon, rotis off the tawa when you sit down.

Everything is served family style, in the middle of the table, meant to be passed and shared.

The Family

Recipes taught by hand, kept by memory

Owner Ronika Singh learned every dish on this menu in her family's kitchen, measured in handfuls and pinches. The chhole are her mother's. The butter chicken is the house recipe her family has argued over and perfected for decades. Her rule is simple: cook it the way it was taught, or leave it off the menu.

The family is all here, including Snoopy, the house shih tzu and honorary sous chef, who supervises the dining room and greets regulars by the door.
